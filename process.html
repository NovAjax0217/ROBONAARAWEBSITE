HOW WE BUILD

Building a competition robot isn't just about putting parts together. Every stage of the process matters — from the first idea to the final match.

01

RESEARCH

We study the challenge, understand the rules, identify possible strategies, and figure out what the robot needs to accomplish.

02

DESIGN

Ideas become designs. We plan the robot's mechanisms, structure, electronics, and overall architecture.

03

BUILD

The design becomes physical. We assemble the mechanisms, connect electronics, and turn individual components into AJAX.

04

CODE

Software brings the robot to life. We develop and refine the code that controls AJAX and helps it respond reliably.

05

TEST

We test individual systems and the complete robot, finding weaknesses and improving reliability.

06

ITERATE

Something doesn't work? We change it. Testing leads to improvements, and improvements make AJAX better.

07

COMPETE

After all the designing, building, coding, and testing, it's time to put everything together and take ROBONAARA to the field.
