ROBONAARA / ENGINEERING LOG

HOW WE
BUILT AJAX.

AJAX didn't appear fully built on the first attempt. The robot evolved through ideas, designs, mechanisms, electronics, programming, testing and plenty of improvements.

THE
BUILD
PROCESS.

01
PHASE 01

THE IDEA

Every robot starts with an idea. The team first explored what AJAX needed to accomplish and began turning the competition challenge into a practical plan.

02
PHASE 02

DESIGN & PLANNING

Ideas were converted into designs. The team planned mechanisms, structure, components and the different systems that would eventually work together as AJAX.

03
PHASE 03

BUILDING AJAX

The design became a physical robot. Components and mechanisms were assembled carefully, turning the plans into something that could actually move and perform.

04
PHASE 04

ELECTRONICS

Motors, controllers, wiring and other electronic systems were connected and tested so that the physical mechanisms of AJAX could respond properly.

05
PHASE 05

PROGRAMMING

Code connected the team's strategy to the robot's behaviour. The programming was refined so AJAX could respond reliably to the team's controls and decisions.

06
PHASE 06

TEST & DEBUG

Nothing works perfectly on the first try. The team tested AJAX repeatedly, found problems, fixed them and improved the robot's performance through iteration.

07
PHASE 07

COMPETITION READY

After designing, building, programming and testing, AJAX is prepared to leave the workshop and take ROBONAARA's work onto the competition field.
