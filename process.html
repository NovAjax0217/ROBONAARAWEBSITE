// HOW WE BUILD AJAX

THE PROCESS

From the first idea to the final match, ROBONAARA works through every stage together. Engineering, coding, testing and strategy all come together to create AJAX.

01

IDEATE

Every build starts with an idea. We discuss the challenge, analyse the requirements and brainstorm different ways AJAX could solve the problem.

02

DESIGN

We turn our ideas into designs, mechanisms and possible solutions. The team considers strength, movement, efficiency and reliability.

03

BUILD

Components become a real robot. We assemble mechanisms, electronics and structural parts while constantly checking every connection.

04

CODE

Programming gives AJAX its intelligence. We write, test and refine the code so the robot responds accurately to our controls and strategy.

05

TEST

Nothing is ready after the first attempt. We repeatedly test AJAX, identify weaknesses, fix problems and improve performance.

06

OPTIMISE

Small improvements can make a huge difference. We tune the robot, refine mechanisms, improve code and make our strategy more efficient.

07

COMPETE

The final step is the field. With AJAX ready, the entire team works together, communicates, adapts and gives everything during the match.
